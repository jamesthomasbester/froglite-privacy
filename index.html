FrogLite Privacy Policy

Developer: Spaghetti Development
Effective date: 26 November 2026

Summary

FrogLite does not collect personal information itself, and the whole game works offline. The only online feature is the optional Endless leaderboard, which uses Google Play Games. If you never sign in to Play Games, nothing about you leaves your device.

Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect any information. FrogLite has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics of its own. The app uses the internet only for the optional Google Play Games leaderboard, and asks for no other device permissions.

Google Play Games (optional leaderboard)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, FrogLite sends your best Endless result to Google to run the worldwide leaderboard. The score records the wave you reached, the Difficulty, your bug count and which frog you played. The leaderboard shows it next to your Play Games name and avatar. Signing in is optional: the game is fully playable without it.

This information is handled by Google, not by us. We do not receive your email address, contacts or any other account details. Google also collects some diagnostic and usage data to run Play Games. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

You control your Play Games profile's visibility and can delete your Play Games data, including leaderboard scores, from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked frogs, achievements, best results and settings) is saved in a file on your device so you can continue playing. This file never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else (only your best Endless score goes to Google Play Games, if you sign in, as described above). Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third parties

FrogLite does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. Its only third-party service is Google Play Games, used for the optional leaderboard (see above). Google Play may also collect information when you download the app; that is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

FrogLite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
