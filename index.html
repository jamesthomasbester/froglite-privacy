FrogLite Privacy Policy

Developer: Spaghetti Development
Effective date: 21 November 2026

Summary

FrogLite does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. FrogLite has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not request internet access or any other device permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked frogs, achievements, best results and settings) is saved in a file on your device so you can continue playing. This file never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third parties

FrogLite does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or data-collection services. Google Play may collect information when you download the app; that is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

FrogLite does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
